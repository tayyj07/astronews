## Solana

- Solana validators passed SGP-0002 ("Double Disinflation") with 67% support — just above the 66.667% threshold — in the network's first-ever on-chain governance vote. The proposal doubles the annual disinflation rate from 15% to 30%, putting Solana on track to hit its 1.5% terminal inflation target in ~2.8 years instead of ~5.7 years. The vote came down to the wire, with two large validators flipping position in the final minutes. Implementation is expected to begin in October, with full activation in H1 2027. ([COINOTAG](https://en.coinotag.com/solana-validators-pass-double-disinflation-67-support-governance-vote))

- CryptoSlate reports the vote hinged on late position switches by Kraken and Galaxy Digital, and that the governance debate now shifts to technical execution — specifically how the new disinflation schedule will be encoded and activated. The tally closed at 176.3M SOL for, 66.2M against, 20.6M abstaining. ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/solana-sgp-0002-passes-with-176-million-sol-as-governance-debate-shifts-to-technical-execution/))
