## Ethereum

- Five former Ethereum Foundation researchers — Ansgar Dietrichs, Barnabé Monnot, Caspar Schwarz-Schilling, Josh Rudolf, and Julian Ma — launched **Ethlabs**, an independent nonprofit R&D organisation backed by Bitmine, SharpLink, and Joe Lubin to ready Ethereum for institutional-scale adoption; funders hold no authority over the research agenda. ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/405694/bitmine-sharplink-and-joe-lubin-back-ethlabs-nonprofit-to-advance-ethereums-next-phase-of-growth))

- Ethlabs' initial work targets faster settlement, native asset issuance, cross-chain interoperability, mainnet capacity, and research into ETH's monetary properties — framing itself as the infrastructure layer for the "institutional supercycle." ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ethlabs-founded-by-former-ethereum-foundation-contributors-and-funded-by-bitmine-sharplink-and-joe-lubin-launches-to-accelerate-ethereums-institutional-supercycle-302806705.html))
