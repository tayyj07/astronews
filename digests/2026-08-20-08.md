## Stablecoins

- Wyoming's state-issued stablecoin WYST officially launched today at the Wyoming Blockchain Symposium in Jackson, becoming the first U.S. state-issued payment stablecoin to go live on mainnet; the token is backed by cash, U.S. Treasuries, and repo agreements with a 102% reserve requirement. ([Paul Hastings](https://www.paulhastings.com/insights/crypto-policy-tracker/wyoming-launches-stablecoin-sec-chair-discusses-tokenization-and-congress-eyes-market-structure-legislation))
- The U.S. Treasury's first GENIUS Act proposed rule is now open for a 60-day public comment period, setting out federal definitions for who may issue and sell payment stablecoins; large foreign issuers such as Tether would need a $47B remediation if the proposed reserve standards are adopted. ([Coinpaprika](https://coinpaprika.com/news/treasury-opens-first-genius-act-rule/))
