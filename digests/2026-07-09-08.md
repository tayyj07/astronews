## Claude design news

- Anthropic's identity and age verification system goes live on July 8 for flagged consumer accounts (Free, Pro, Max plans): users may be asked for a government-issued ID + live selfie, with age checks via Yoti and identity checks via Persona. Anthropic states the data will not be used for model training. ([Cyber Insider](https://cyberinsider.com/anthropic-to-introduce-age-and-id-checks-for-claude-users-on-july-8/))
- Anthropic frames the rollout as a safety measure targeting a "small subset of users" whose accounts trigger review thresholds, not a blanket requirement — but it is the first formal biometric-adjacent policy on the platform. ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/technology/anthropic-doubles-down-on-safety-claude-id-checks))

## Solana

- Clearstream — Deutsche Börse Group's post-trade infrastructure provider — expanded its regulated crypto custody offering on July 8 to include SOL, XRP, ADA, AVAX, LTC, and XLM, joining BTC and ETH already on the platform. The expansion is driven by demand for MiCA-compliant institutional custody in Europe, with sub-custody provided by Crypto Finance (another Deutsche Börse Group entity holding a MiCAR license). ([Crypto News](https://crypto.news/clearstream-expands-crypto-custody-with-xrp-sol-ada-avax/))
- FX News Group flags that Clearstream's move broadens the scope of regulated DeFi exposure for European institutional investors navigating MiCA requirements. ([FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/institutional/clearstream-broadens-scope-of-its-crypto-custody-offering/))

- USDT supply reached ~$158–160 billion at Q2-end (all-time high), but Dune analytics data shows USDT and USDC are diverging into distinct roles: USDT dominates cross-border payments and exchange liquidity, while USDC leads in DeFi settlement and institutional on-chain use — the two stablecoins are no longer direct competitors. ([Stablecoin Insider](https://stablecoininsider.org/usdt-q2-2026-report-supply-regulation-and-the-two-tier-market/))
