## Ethereum

- Glamsterdam reached its final devnet phase this week, locking in ten EIPs — headlined by ePBS (EIP-7732, enshrining Proposer-Builder Separation) and Block-Level Access Lists (EIP-7928, enabling parallel transaction processing) — with a 200 million gas-limit floor and mainnet activation targeted for H2 2026. On a two-to-four-month testnet cadence, mainnet would land September–December 2026. ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/blockchains/ethereum-glamsterdam-final-devnet-200m-gas-limit-target))

- The final devnet milestone clears the path for 78% gas fee reduction and ~10,000 TPS throughput, making Glamsterdam the most significant Ethereum protocol change since the Merge. ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/ethereum-glamsterdam-upgrade-final-devnet/))

## Stablecoins

- Fidelity Investments launched its **Reserves Digital Fund** on June 18 — a GENIUS Act-compliant money market fund holding short-dated Treasuries, overnight repos, and government MMFs — designed to hold regulated reserves for stablecoin issuers. The product enters a race with State Street's recently launched stablecoin reserve fund, targeting what analysts estimate could become a $4T market. ([Cryptonomist](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/06/18/fidelity-stablecoin-reserve-fund/))

- Crypto News notes Fidelity's Reserves Digital Fund is the fifth stablecoin-reserve money market product to file in the US, signalling rapid institutionalisation of the GENIUS Act reserve compliance market just days after the Senate passed the bill 68–30. ([Crypto News](https://crypto.news/fidelity-launches-stablecoin-reserve-fund-under-genius-act-framework/))
