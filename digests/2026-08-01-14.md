Key EIPs: EIP-7732 (enshrined Proposer-Builder Separation, projected to cut MEV extraction by ~70%) and EIP-7928 (Block-Level Access Lists enabling parallel tx processing, pushing the gas limit from 60M to 200M CU and targeting ~10,000 TPS with an estimated 78% L1 fee reduction). ## Hyperliquid

- HIP-4 permissionless outcome markets are now **live on testnet**: any developer can deploy a prediction market by staking just 100 HYPE (no auction, no gas fee), with a limit of 10 active outcomes and 50 deployments per day per deployer. Mainnet deployment will require 500,000 HYPE staked for six months. ([Coinpedia](https://coinpedia.org/news/hyperliquid-launches-hip-4-testnet-as-hype-price-tests-53/))

([Cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/hyperliquid-hip-4-permissionless-prediction-markets/))
