## Stablecoins

- **BIS General Manager Pablo Hernández de Cos** told the Jackson Hole symposium stablecoins are "not a credible means of payment at scale," citing the absence of par-redemption enforcement and fragmented secondary-market exchange as structural weaknesses. ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/stablecoins-not-credible-payments-scale-bis-chief))
- Stablecoin market cap held at ~$290.5B (+0.2%) while broader crypto shed 2.9%; de Cos's Jackson Hole remarks are generating significant macro and crypto media coverage. ([The Crypto Times](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/08/29/bis-chief-says-stablecoins-are-not-ready-for-payments-at-scale/))

## Stablecoins Development in the TradFi Space

- De Cos called tokenized deposits — account-based bank liabilities settled via central bank accounts — the preferred path for bulk day-to-day payments, arguing they preserve monetary "singleness" that stablecoins cannot. ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/digital-payments/2026/bis-general-manager-says-tokenized-deposits-beat-stablecoins-digital-payments/))
- Despite BIS skepticism, European (ING, UniCredit, KBC, DekaBank), Asian (Japan megabanks), and now US banks (BofA, Wells Fargo, Santander) are actively building stablecoin infrastructure — widening the regulatory vs. market divergence. ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/bis-chief-stablecoins-lack-credibility-payments/))

## Cross-Cutting Themes

- **BIS vs. banks**: The BIS is explicitly endorsing tokenized deposits while private banks race to issue their own stablecoins; this divergence is likely to dominate fintech/DeFi regulatory discourse in Q4 2026.
- **On-chain governance matures**: Solana's SGP votes demonstrate validator governance works at scale but is fragile — a 67% pass on a 66.67% threshold after last-minute institutional flips highlights the outsized power of concentrated stake; quorum/veto design debates will follow.
