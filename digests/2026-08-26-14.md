## Stablecoins

- 39 US state banking associations announced the "BankChain Alliance," a plan to build a 24/7, bank-governed and permissioned nationwide blockchain network by 2027, explicitly aimed at competing with crypto stablecoins as the CLARITY Act remains stalled in the Senate. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/08/25/u-s-state-banking-associations-plan-to-launch-their-own-nationwide-blockchain-network))
- Total stablecoin market cap stands at $308 billion as of mid-August 2026, up 14.3% year-over-year; Treasury's GENIUS Act implementation sets January 2027 as the issuance ban effective date for unlicensed payment stablecoin issuers. ([Coinpedia](https://coinpedia.org/news/banks-unveil-bankchain-alliance-to-rival-crypto-stablecoins/))
