## Claude Design News

- Anthropic published a postmortem tracing six weeks of Claude Code quality regressions to three independent product-layer changes shipped between March and April 2026: a reasoning-effort downgrade from high to medium, a caching bug that progressively erased the model's own thinking on every turn, and a system-prompt verbosity limit that cut coding quality by ~3%. All three have been resolved as of v2.1.116, and usage limits have been reset for all subscribers. ([InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/05/anthropic-claude-code-postmortem/))

## Ethereum

- Ethereum's Glamsterdam upgrade has slipped from its June 2026 target to Q3 2026; the gas limit is now set at 200 million (vs. the current 60M ceiling), with a 30-day security audit cycle and dual testnet runs on Holesky and Sepolia required before a mainnet date is announced. ([CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/ethereum-glamsterdam-upgrade-pushed-q3))
- Glamsterdam's two headline features—enshrined Proposer-Builder Separation (ePBS, EIP-7732) and Block-Level Access Lists for parallel execution (EIP-7928)—are projected to triple Ethereum's Layer 1 throughput and cut gas fees by up to 78%. ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/blockchains/glamsterdam-upgrade-set-to-triple-ethereum-s-execution-capacity))
