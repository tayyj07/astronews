## Stablecoins

- The **US Federal Reserve proposed stablecoin rules** under the GENIUS Act on September 24: issuers supervised by the Fed must maintain **1:1 backing** with permissible reserve assets (primarily short-term T-bills), honour redemptions within **two business days**, and carry standardised capital against operational and credit risks. ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/379274/federal-reserve-unveils-stablecoin-rules-on-reserves-and-capital))
- The Fed simultaneously released a tailored application process for Board-supervised banks seeking to issue stablecoins; comment period closes 60 days after Federal Register publication. ([BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/markets/crypto/2026/09/24/us-federal-reserve-proposes-new-stablecoin-rules/))
- The CFTC published updated FAQs on September 24 clarifying that futures commission merchants (FCMs) may apply customer-deposited **payment stablecoins as margin** and to secure debit/deficit balances. ([Lowenstein Sandler](https://www.lowenstein.com/news-insights/newsletters/crypto-brief-september-24-2026))
