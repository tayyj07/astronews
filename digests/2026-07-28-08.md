## Claude Design News

- Roughly 600 Claude shared conversations were indexed by Google and Bing, exposing sensitive user data including crypto wallet keys, source code, and corporate documents; Anthropic's "Create public link" feature generated public URLs that lacked `noindex` directives, allowing search crawlers to reach content users believed was private. ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/uh-oh-some-claude-shared-conversations-and-artifacts-appear-to-be-indexed-and-publicly-accessible-on-google-search))
