## stablecoins

- U.S. Bancorp completed its first live cross-border USBDC stablecoin transaction on the Stellar network on September 9, testing the full lifecycle (mint, pay, redeem, freeze, clawback) between its North American and European entities — making it one of the first major US banks to run a dollar-backed stablecoin on a public blockchain in a live setting. ([Yahoo Finance](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/us-bancorp-executes-first-live-115331474.html))

([CoinFomania](https://coinfomania.com/mas-moves-to-regulate-systemic-stablecoins-as-global/))
