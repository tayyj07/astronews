## Claude Design News

- Anthropic launched **Claude Duet** in research preview on October 21, a proactive AI agent for Pro and Max subscribers in the US. Running on Opus 5.5, it monitors connected apps autonomously, sends a morning brief, and checks in every few hours — acting on routine tasks without prompting and requiring explicit approval for anything consequential (sending messages, payments, signing documents). ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/21/anthropic-claude-duet/))
- Claude Duet runs cloud-side even when the user's device is off; securities trading and fund transfers between accounts are fully prohibited. It competes with Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots as a persistent ambient agent. ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-unveils-claude-duet-ai-agent/))
- Separately, Anthropic took Claude Docs, Slides, and Design out of beta on all plans (including Free) and launched Claude Dashboards and Motion; Enterprise admins get these enabled by default from October 15. ([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/anthropic-launches-claude-dashboards-and-motion-takes-docs-out-of-beta/))

## Solana

- Solana's Alpenglow upgrade is live on mainnet-beta as of October 21 (slot 459,653,000, epoch 1064); initial Foundation dashboard readings show **202ms finality** vs. the 150ms design target, with 654 validators participating in the handoff in 900ms. ([AltcoinBuzz](https://www.altcoinbuzz.io/solana-activates-alpenglow-targeting-150ms-finality))
- Co-founder Anatoly Yakovenko has asked for the "mainnet-beta" label to be removed from the explorer now that Alpenglow is live; no formal change announced yet. SOL trades near $105–108. ([CoinGabbar](https://www.coingabbar.com/en/solana-news-alpenglow-live-mainnet-finality))
- Post-launch coverage notes the upgrade delivers an 85x finality improvement; META is now live on Solana through Sunrise (issued by Backpack Securities), and GMX proposed using Solana-side revenue for token buybacks. ([CoinPaper](https://coinpaper.com/37686/solana-activates-alpenglow-aiming-to-make-transactions-final-nearly-85-times-faster))

## Stablecoins

- The OCC's GENIUS Act reaction pieces land: the American Bankers Association called the final rule's removal of the rewards-presumption ban a **loophole**, arguing issuers can deliver economically equivalent yield while technically avoiding the statutory prohibition. ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/american-bankers-association-occ-genius-rules-stablecoins))
- U.Today frames the rules as outlawing direct "crypto dollar" interest while leaving exchanges a rewards loophole — shifting the debate to regulatory interpretation of what structures are permissible. ([U.Today](https://u.today/stablecoin-earning-not-dead-us-outlaws-crypto-dollar-interest))
- Circle and Coinbase shares each rose 4%+ on the final rule news; the White House said stablecoin rewards won't materially impact community banks. Rules take effect January 18, 2027. ([The Crypto Times](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/10/21/occ-finalizes-first-genius-act-stablecoin-rule-drops-rewards-presumption/))
