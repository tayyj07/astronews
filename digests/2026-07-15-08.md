## Claude design news

- Anthropic launched **Claude for Teachers** on July 14, giving verified US K-12 educators one free year of premium Claude access—including Claude Code and Cowork—with curriculum linked to academic standards across all 50 states and a FERPA-aligned data agreement; student data will not be used to train Claude models. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-teachers))
- The launch puts Anthropic alongside Google, OpenAI, and Khan Academy in an AI race for classrooms; the platform integrates with ASSISTments, Canva Education, MagicSchool, TeachFX, and eight other EdTech tools. ([Chalkbeat](https://www.chalkbeat.org/2026/07/14/anthropic-launches-claude-for-teachers-as-ai-companies-battle-for-classrooms/))
- Verified educators can apply for access through June 30, 2027; Anthropic co-created an AI fluency course with Teach for America as a companion resource. ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/danfitzpatrick/2026/07/14/anthropic-launches-ai-for-teachers/))

## Hyperliquid

- The SEC Crypto Task Force held a formal meeting on July 14 with Hyperliquid Policy Center CEO Jake Chervinsky, Highland Labs representatives, and Sullivan & Cromwell lawyers to discuss regulation of decentralised perpetual derivatives and on-chain market infrastructure—marking Hyperliquid's first direct regulatory engagement with the SEC. ([The Crypto Times](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/07/15/hyperliquid-meets-sec-crypto-task-force-over-u-s-regulations/))
- The meeting confirmed regulatory dialogue is ongoing but does not constitute approval of HYPE, HIP-3 products, or US-trader access. ([Bloomingbit](https://en.bloomingbit.io/feed/news/116247))
