## Claude Design News

- Anthropic launched **Claude Opus 5** on July 24 — a new frontier model priced at $5/M input and $25/M output tokens that "approaches the capabilities of Fable 5 at half the price." The model ships with a 1 M-token context window, per-turn effort controls (low / medium / high / xhigh), and a safety fallback to Opus 4.8; Anthropic expects it to become the default for most everyday office work. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/anthropic-launches-opus-5/))
- Opus 5 outperforms Fable 5 on several benchmarks including agentic coding and novel problem solving, while matching it on knowledge work and multidisciplinary reasoning; IPO preparations are reportedly underway later in 2026. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/anthropic-debuts-opus-5-model-as-company-preps-for-ipo-later-this-year-170000070.html))
- Bloomberg notes the key differentiator is the user-facing effort toggle, giving enterprises fine-grained cost-vs-capability control per task rather than routing requests across separate model tiers. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/anthropic-unveils-more-cost-efficient-model-for-everyday-tasks))
- Fortune's writeup highlights Opus 5's positioning: filling the gap between the everyday Haiku tier and the frontier Fable 5, targeting enterprise deployments that found Fable 5 too expensive for bulk workloads. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/24/anthropic-debuts-claude-opus-5-with-feature-that-lets-users-toggle-between-cost-and-capability/))
