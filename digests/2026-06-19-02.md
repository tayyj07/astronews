## Stablecoins

- The Federal Reserve proposed Thursday that permitted payment stablecoin issuers (PPSIs) must collect each new customer's legal name, date of birth, physical address, and government-issued ID before account opening—extending bank-style AML/CIP discipline to stablecoins. Secondary-market transfers not involving the issuer directly are excluded. ([Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/federal-reserve-moves-to-close-stablecoin))
- The Crypto Times noted that a 60-day comment window means final CIP rules are unlikely before 2027, creating a potential compliance gap ahead of the GENIUS Act's July 18 implementation deadline. ([The Crypto Times](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/06/18/us-moves-to-tighten-stablecoin-id-rules-under-genius-act/))
