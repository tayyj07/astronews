([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/05/11/ronin-set-to-transition-to-ethereum-layer-2-from-independent-sidechain))

([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/400732/from-hack-to-op-stack-ronin-to-migrate-from-gaming-sidechain-to-ethereum-layer-2-four-years-after-lazarus-attack))

## Stablecoins

- ABA CEO Rob Nichols sent an emergency letter to every US bank CEO on Sunday urging "immediate engagement" against the Clarity Act's stablecoin yield loophole, days before the Senate Banking Committee's May 14 markup; the ABA warns permitting any yield-like rewards could scale the stablecoin market from ~$300B to $2T and drain bank deposit funding. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/05/11/banking-groups-escalate-fight-over-stablecoin-yield-ahead-of-senate-vote))
- Senate Banking Committee has confirmed May 14 for the CLARITY Act markup; Senate Democrats — including Senators Warner and Schatz — hold the swing votes as the bank lobby mounts its last-ditch resistance to the Tillis-Alsobrooks yield compromise. ([Unchained Crypto](https://unchainedcrypto.com/senate-banking-committee-sets-may-14-clarity-act-markup-as-bank-lobby-mounts-last-ditch-push/))
- Crypto Times breaks down the core disputed language: the Tillis-Alsobrooks draft bans holding-based stablecoin rewards (seen as equivalent to bank deposit interest) but permits activity-based rewards earned through payments; banking groups argue both forms are functionally equivalent. ([Crypto Times](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/05/11/clarity-act-stablecoin-rules-spark-last-minute-bank-lobby-blitz/))

## Stablecoins development in the tradfi space
