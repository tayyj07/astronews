## Ethereum

- Ethereum's Glamsterdam hard fork entered its final devnet stage, locking in ten EIPs headlined by Enshrined PBS (EIP-7732) and Block-Level Access Lists (EIP-7928); a 200M gas-limit floor is targeted post-mainnet, with end-of-August 2026 as an aspirational activation date. ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/blockchains/ethereum-glamsterdam-final-devnet-200m-gas-limit-target))
- Glamsterdam's parallel execution framework could roughly triple L1 capacity and cut standard ETH transfer costs up to 71%; two-to-four months of public-testnet seasoning is expected before mainnet, targeting H2 2026. ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/ethereum-glamsterdam-upgrade-final-devnet/))
