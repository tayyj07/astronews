## Stablecoins

- The Clarity Act cleared the Senate Banking Committee 15-9, advancing to the full Senate floor; two Democrats (Gallego, Alsobrooks) crossed the aisle. The bill still needs 60 Senate votes and House passage before going to President Trump. ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/clarity-act-clears-major-senate-committee-hurdle-advances-to-the-full-senate-floor/))
- The American Bankers Association published its post-vote statement, signalling continued opposition and calling for further refinements to the stablecoin yield language before the Senate floor vote. ([ABA Banking Journal](https://bankingjournal.aba.com/2026/05/senate-banking-committee-advances-clarity-act/))

## Stablecoins Development in the TradFi Space

- The Bank Policy Institute issued a formal statement on the Clarity Act committee vote, calling for changes to the bill's stablecoin provisions — particularly around yield parity between bank-issued tokenised deposits and non-bank stablecoin issuers. ([Bank Policy Institute](https://bpi.com/banking-trades-statement-on-senate-banking-committee-vote-to-advance-clarity-act/))
- FinTech Weekly analysis finds that, as currently drafted, the Clarity Act favours banks on stablecoin yield: non-bank issuers face tighter restrictions than bank-issued tokenised deposit products. ([FinTech Weekly](https://www.fintechweekly.com/news/clarity-act-banks-winning-stablecoin-yield-2026))

## Cross-cutting Themes

The Clarity Act's committee passage is the thread connecting this window's stablecoin and TradFi coverage: the 15-9 vote moves the bill to the Senate floor while banking-industry bodies immediately signalled ongoing resistance — centred on stablecoin yield parity — that could shape floor negotiations through August.
