## Stablecoins

- A consortium of major US banks — including JPMorgan, Bank of America, Citi, and Wells Fargo — has formally joined forces to build a shared tokenized-deposit network, operated by The Clearing House and targeting a 2027 live date, to counter the rising threat from private stablecoins and the Open USD consortium. ([Banking Exchange](https://www.bankingexchange.com/news-feed/item/10640-major-us-banks-join-forces-on-tokenized-deposit-network))
- OCC Comptroller Jonathan Gould said the OCC will finalize GENIUS Act implementing rules for bank-issued stablecoins by November, providing clearer guidance ahead of the statute's effective date of January 2027. ([MLex](https://www.mlex.com/mlex/amp/articles/2515591))

## Stablecoins Development in TradFi

- A new analysis argues stablecoin regulation is fundamentally about preserving US dollar primacy in cross-border payments rather than consumer protection — framing the GENIUS Act, OCC rulemaking, and FASB cash-equivalence criteria as coordinated levers to keep dollar-backed stablecoins dominant globally. ([crypto.news](https://crypto.news/stablecoin-regulation-not-consumer-protection-about-the-dollar/))
- The Open USD consortium — backed by Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, Coinbase, BlackRock, BNY, Google, Shopify, and Standard Chartered — is on track to launch a shared private stablecoin in 2026, a full year ahead of the bank-led tokenized-deposit network, widening the race between TradFi incumbents and fintech-led initiatives. ([Banking Exchange](https://www.bankingexchange.com/news-feed/item/10640-major-us-banks-join-forces-on-tokenized-deposit-network))
