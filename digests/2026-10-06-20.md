## Stablecoins

- Stablecoin payments firm Rain filed an OCC application for Rain National Trust Bank (New York), seeking to custody digital assets, hold stablecoin reserves, and issue dollar-pegged stablecoins under the GENIUS Act — the application comes three days after community bankers sued to challenge OCC crypto trust charters. ([Parameter](https://parameter.io/stablecoin-firm-rain-files-for-federal-trust-bank-charter-amid-industry-legal-battle))
- FDIC has aligned its proposed GENIUS Act stablecoin rulemaking with the OCC's framework, with the two agencies converging on standards covering reserve assets, risk management, custody, and capital backstop requirements for payment stablecoin issuers. ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/regulation/2026/fdic-aligns-with-occ-to-solidify-genius-act-rules/))
