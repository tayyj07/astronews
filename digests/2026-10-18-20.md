## Solana

- Solana's Alpenglow consensus upgrade activates at epoch 1064, targeting ~15:00 UTC on October 21; validators must be running Agave 4.3.0 or higher and hold at least 0.8 SOL in their vote account — operators missing these conditions at epoch boundary stop voting and earning rewards in epoch 1065. ([CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/solana-alpenglow-epoch-1064-staking-check/))
- Solana Foundation officially confirmed the October 21 mainnet activation for Alpenglow, which replaces TowerBFT with the Votor protocol targeting finality in ~150ms — a ~98.8% reduction from the current ~12.8 seconds. ([TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/technology/34321))

## Stablecoins

- A new BIS working paper (WP 1382, "Are Stablecoins and Bank Deposits Substitutes?") finds that a 1 basis point rise in bank deposit rates correlates with ~3 percentage points less stablecoin market-cap growth over ten weeks — the effect is larger for USDC (~4.8pp) than USDT (~2pp), suggesting dollar-pegged stablecoins compete directly with interest-bearing deposits. ([CoinPaprika](https://coinpaprika.com/news/banks-hold-quiet-lever-stablecoin-growth/))
- Secondary analysis of the BIS paper notes that banks may already hold a structural competitive lever over stablecoin adoption without needing regulatory intervention — simply paying savers more could meaningfully slow stablecoin growth. ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/bis-paper-banks-slow-stablecoin-growth-rates/))

## Stablecoins — development in the TradFi space

- The BIS deposit-rate paper has direct TradFi implications: incumbent banks can compete with stablecoin issuers through conventional monetary tools, undercutting arguments that stablecoins are structurally immune to bank competition. ([DeFi Prime](https://defiprime.com/bis-links-deposit-rates-to-slower-stablecoin-growth))
- Marqeta and Coinbase announced a partnership to enable stablecoin-backed payment cards for enterprise clients: Coinbase handles on-chain custody, Base provides settlement infrastructure, and Marqeta manages card issuance and network relationships — supported stablecoins, fees, and launch date not yet disclosed. ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/coinbase-marqeta-stablecoin-payment-cards/))
