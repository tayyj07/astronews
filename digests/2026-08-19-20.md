## Stablecoins

- FASB proposed a three-test framework for stablecoins to qualify as cash equivalents under U.S. GAAP: (1) on-demand contractual redemption right directly with the issuer, (2) redemption for a known cash amount, and (3) at least 1:1 segregated reserves in short-term, highly liquid assets — secondary-market liquidity alone would not suffice; comment period runs through November 19. ([CoinSpectator](https://coinspectator.com/cryptonews/2026/08/19/fasb-sets-3-tests-for-stablecoins-to-qualify-as-cash/))
- Multiple outlets including CoinTelegraph and CryptoBriefing confirmed the proposed Accounting Standards Update would add illustrative examples to the existing cash-equivalent definition, resolving the inconsistent GAAP treatment that has blocked corporates from booking compliant stablecoins as near-cash. ([CoinTelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/us-accounting-board-fasb-proposes-conditions-for-stablecoins-as-cash-equivalents)) ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/fasb-stablecoins-cash-equivalents-accounting/))
