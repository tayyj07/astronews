## Stablecoins

- The Bank Policy Institute published renewed criticism of the OCC's October 21 final GENIUS Act rule, pressing the regulator for explicit guidance on how it will evaluate third-party yield arrangements on a "case-by-case" basis after the ban on direct issuer interest payments was kept. ([TokenPost](https://tokenpost.com/news/regulation/40653))
- NewsBTC covers the bank-lobby pushback in detail, noting TD Cowen flagged the case-by-case standard as a negative for banks because it creates pricing uncertainty for deposit-competing stablecoin products. ([NewsBTC](https://www.newsbtc.com/crypto-regulation/bpi-occ-stablecoin-yield-rewards-clarity))
- CryptoSlate reports that the OCC's final rule quietly removed several reserve safeguards that banks had sought — including a maturity cap on Treasury holdings and a mandated bank-account allocation floor — prompting warnings from bank groups that billions in deposits could shift to stablecoin issuers. ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/treasury-hails-americas-new-stablecoin-system-as-occ-removes-safeguards-banks-wanted-against-deposit-flight/))
