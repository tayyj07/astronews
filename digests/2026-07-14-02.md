## Stablecoins — TradFi Development

- JPMorgan, Bank of America, Citigroup, HSBC and Wells Fargo unveiled a shared tokenised-deposit network targeting a 2027 launch, positioning it as a bank-controlled alternative to stablecoins; the consortium is also lobbying against CLARITY Act provisions that would let stablecoins pay interest, which they see as a direct threat to deposit funding. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-13/wall-street-mounts-pushback-on-trillion-dollar-stablecoin-boom))
- The network will use tokenised deposits — traditional bank deposits represented on a blockchain — rather than fiat-backed stablecoins, keeping funds and credit risk inside the regulated banking system. ([Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/jpmorgan-citi-bofa-and-wells-fargo-plan-2027-tokenized-deposit-network-as-banks-move-to-counter-stablecoins/))
