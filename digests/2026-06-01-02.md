## Stablecoins Development in the TradFi Space

- The Waller–Greene exchange at Dubrovnik frames the 2026 TradFi divide: the Fed sees dollar-backed stablecoins as tools for extending monetary reach globally, while the BoE bets that regulated banks will win by programmabilising existing deposits rather than ceding the field to non-bank issuers such as Circle or Tether. The Dubrovnik central-banker divide is the window's defining story. Waller and Greene represent two credible visions of programmable money's endgame — stablecoin dominance vs. bank-issued tokenized deposits — and their disagreement will shape regulation, market structure, and TradFi product strategy for years ahead.
