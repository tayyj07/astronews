## Claude Design News

- Anthropic launched **Claude Tag**, a Slack-native feature that lets a single Claude agent interact on behalf of users across a channel — monitoring threads, sending contextual alerts, and handing off mid-conversation between team members. Anthropic says ~65% of its own product team's code is now generated via an internal Claude Tag build. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-23/anthropic-wants-claude-to-be-your-new-slack-coworker))

## Ethereum

- The Ethereum Foundation confirmed a **20% workforce reduction** — 54 positions eliminated — alongside a **40% budget cut** for 2026. Vitalik Buterin said annual spend will drop from ~15% to ~5% of assets by 2030, shifting to an endowment-style model. The org is reorganizing into five clusters: protocol, access, user, community, and institutional. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/06/23/ethereum-foundation-cuts-20-of-staff-amid-leadership-exodus))
- The cuts follow **eight senior departures** over five months; affected staff receive severance and career-transition grants. ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/371864/ethereum-foundation-cuts-20-of-workforce-in-leaner-reorganization))
- ETH is down 44% YTD despite record on-chain activity — the contradiction between shrinking Foundation resources and growing network usage is sharpening. ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/ethereum-foundation-cuts-20-of-staff-as-eth-sinks-44-ytd-despite-record-usage/))

## Stablecoins

- The **BIS 2026 Annual Economic Report** issued a blunt warning: stablecoins "fall short as money." With ~$320B in supply (99.4% USD-pegged), the BIS flags fire-sale risk from mass redemptions, AML gaps on permissionless chains, and potential "dollarization" of emerging markets. ([BusinessDay](https://www.businessday.co.za/economy/2026-06-24-bis-says-stablecoins-fall-short-as-money-and-threaten-global-financial-integrity/)) ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/crypto/markets/worlds-central-bank-issues-blunt-warning-on-stablecoin-boom))

## Stablecoins Development in the TradFi Space

- The BIS recommends resolving stablecoin risk through **integration into the regulated banking system** — tokenized money anchored in central bank reserves, rather than standalone stablecoin issuance. The report effectively endorses the tokenized-deposit model for systemic financial infrastructure. ([BIS Annual Report, Ch. III](https://www.bis.org/publ/arpdf/ar2026e3.htm)) ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/global-banking-body-issues-blunt-043000731.html))

## Cross-cutting themes

Two institutions are pulling back in different ways: the Ethereum Foundation is cutting headcount and pivoting to lean, mission-focused clusters — while the BIS is pushing stablecoins to defer to central-bank-anchored tokenized deposits. Both moves signal that the open, permissionless model faces pressure to accommodate institutional rails.
