## Stablecoins

- Analysts argue that institutional stablecoin volume is structurally capped until confidentiality is built in: on-chain transparency exposes supplier relationships, pricing strategy, and treasury positions — problems programmable "selective disclosure" addresses, and which the GENIUS Act's AML obligations make more urgent. ([BeinCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/stablecoins-need-confidentiality-to-move-institutional-volume/))
- PYMNTS frames the same structural barrier as the "privacy problem institutions can't ignore," noting that the lack of confidentiality is now the primary obstacle blocking large-scale stablecoin adoption in wholesale payments. ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/cryptocurrency/2026/the-privacy-problem-institutions-cant-ignore-in-stablecoins/))

## Stablecoins development in the tradfi space

- MUFG, Mizuho, and SMBC signed a joint MOU on June 10 to issue a yen stablecoin targeting live corporate transactions by March 2027, built on MUFG's Progmat platform (supports Ethereum, Polygon, Avalanche, Cosmos); their explicit goal is to counter USDC/USDT dominance in Asian digital settlements. ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/404212/japans-three-megabanks-stablecoin-march-2027))
- Follow-on reporting notes the consortium is targeting ¥1 trillion in B2B stablecoin volume by 2028 under an initiative called "Project Pax." ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/370615/japans-largest-banks-plan-joint-stablecoin-launch-by-march-2027))
