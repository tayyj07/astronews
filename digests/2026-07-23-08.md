## Claude Design News

- Anthropic launched **"Record a Skill"** (Teach Claude a Skill), a new Cowork feature that lets Pro/Max/Team users record their screen and narrate a workflow; Claude converts the recording into a reusable, automatable skill — cutting repetitive prompting for recurring tasks like reports and file organisation. ([Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/07/22/anthropic-teach-claude-screen-recording-feature/))
- Multiple outlets covered the launch, noting the feature is available in the Claude desktop app's Cowork interface and uses mouse clicks, keystrokes, and voice commentary to construct a structured skill definition. ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/claude-skill-teaching-feature/))

## Stablecoins

- **Balance (BLC) stablecoin collapsed 99%** to ~$0.0014 after an attacker exploited a BTCB price oracle on BNB Chain, tricking the lending contract into accepting an abnormally low bitcoin price and instantly liquidating collateralised vaults; ~$912 K was drained, largely from governance entity 42DAO. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/07/22/balance-stablecoin-collapses-99-after-usd1-million-exploit-drains-its-bitcoin-vaults))
- Security firm SlowMist noted the attack is representative of a 2026 DeFi trend: exploits targeting **oracles and surrounding infrastructure** rather than contract logic, erasing nearly all of BLC's ~$3.5 M market cap in a single strike. ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/balance-stablecoin-collapse/))
