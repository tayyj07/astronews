## Stablecoins

- Tomorrow (July 18) is the GENIUS Act's statutory one-year rulemaking deadline, yet none of the six mandated federal agencies (OCC, FDIC, NCUA, Treasury, FinCEN, OFAC) has published final stablecoin rules; a miss creates no fallback and leaves new federal applicants, foreign issuers, and state-qualified issuers in legal limbo. ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/genius-made-stablecoins-legal-july-18-decides-which-stablecoins-stay-competitive/))

([Thirdweb](https://blog.thirdweb.com/genius-act-countdown-7-days-until-us-stablecoin-rules-reshape-the-market/))
