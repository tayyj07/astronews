## Stablecoins

- StablR's EURR and USDR stablecoins lose their pegs after an attacker compromised a single key in the 1-of-3 minting multisig, minting 8.35 M USDR and 4.5 M EURR and swapping ~$10.4 M for ~1,115 ETH on DEXes; EURR fell to ~$0.88 and USDR to ~$0.70. Blockaid attributed the breach to a key-management failure rather than any smart-contract bug. ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/euro-and-usd-stablecoins-depeg-amid-ongoing-28m-stablr-exploit))
- ZachXBT flags StablR-linked contracts hacked for over $3 M as EURR and USDR crash ~20%; incident revives concerns about minimal-multisig governance in stablecoin issuance. ([BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/stablr-stablecoin-depeg-exploit/))
- CryptoBriefing confirms suspected $2.8 M exploit via smart-contract admin takeover; StablR team confirms incident and says remediation is under way. ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/stablecoin-issuer-stablr-hit-suspected-3m-smart-contract-exploit-zachxbt/))
