## Ethereum

- The Ethereum Foundation published a full tier list of 62 EIPs submitted for the Hegotá upgrade, designating FOCIL (EIP-7805) and Frame Transactions (EIP-8141) as the two "must-ship" proposals; EIP-8141 would let wallets pay gas fees in stablecoins like USDC instead of ETH. ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2026-09-08-ethereum-foundation-quantum-resistance-2029-413716))
- Alongside the EIP rankings, the Foundation set December 2029 as the target for making Ethereum's base layer fully quantum-resistant across execution, consensus and data — requiring an average fork cadence of one every 7.2 months from Glamsterdam onwards. ([Cryptonomist](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/09/08/ethereum-hegota-upgrade/))

## Stablecoins

- UBS-led Swiss franc stablecoin CHFD moved from internal testing into a live sandbox on September 8, with infrastructure operator SIX and mobile payments company TWINT joining the existing consortium of UBS, PostFinance, Sygnum, Raiffeisen, Zürcher Kantonalbank, BCV and Swiss Stablecoin AG; the test phase runs through end-2026. ([UBS](https://www.ubs.com/global/en/media/display-page-ndp/en-20260908-stablecoin-sandbox.html))
- The CHFD sandbox is exploring tokenised-asset settlement, programmable payments and cross-border institutional transactions, with an open outcome on whether a commercial launch follows. ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/swiss-chf-stablecoin-sandbox-six-twint/))
