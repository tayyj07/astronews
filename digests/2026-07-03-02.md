## Solana

- Solana activated on-chain governance via Solana Governance Proposals (SGPs): a proposal requires 100,000 SOL staked to the initiating validator to open, 15% of active stake to advance, and a two-thirds supermajority to pass, with tallies recorded on-chain via Merkle proof; the framework introduces "staker sovereignty," giving delegators the ability to override their validator's vote with their own stake-weighted choice. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/07/02/solana-adds-onchain-governance-with-usd7-7-million-sol-needed-to-open-proposals))

- SOL broke above $80 — up over 10% in 24 hours — on the governance news, with active addresses nearing 7M and the 7-day average TPS trending toward a new all-time high of ~1,100; CryptoTimes notes SGPs are separated from technical SIMDs, which remain the engineering pathway for protocol changes. ([CryptoTimes](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/07/02/sol-breaks-above-80-solana-launches-onchain-governance-proposals-sgps/))

- Crypto.news reports that delegators can cast or override votes independently of their validator — a design choice the Solana Foundation calls a significant step toward genuine decentralised governance for the network. ([Crypto.news](https://crypto.news/solana-launches-onchain-governance-with-validator-voting/))
