## Stablecoins

- BIS research across 130+ economies concludes that US dollar-backed stablecoins are "largely unaffected" by capital controls and FX restrictions, as they circulate partly outside the regulatory perimeter; total dollar-stablecoin supply hit $292.6B (up from $253B a year ago) and the BIS warns that dollarization once established is hard to reverse. ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/409193/bis-warns-stablecoins-capital-controls))

## Stablecoins development in the tradfi space

- BIS study flags monetary sovereignty risk for emerging markets: dollar stablecoins are slipping past the capital flow barriers that 130+ governments use to manage their currencies, with the threat most acute where inflation and FX restrictions already push households toward dollar assets. ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/409193/bis-warns-stablecoins-capital-controls))

- **Ethereum regulatory clearing:** The SEC FOIA settlement officially closes the Gensler-era Ethereum enforcement chapter; combined with ongoing Glamsterdam upgrade work, ETH's institutional regulatory overhang is at its lowest point in years.
