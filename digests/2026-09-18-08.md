## Claude Design News

- Anthropic revealed that Claude now "leads" 26% of its internal AI research and development—up from effectively zero at the start of the year—meaning the model can complete most tasks end-to-end from a high-level prompt while a human supervises; AI assists on more than 90% of all R&D work. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-17/anthropic-says-claude-drives-26-of-its-research-and-development))
- The Washington Post frames the same announcement as Claude "taking over the work of building its own successor," the clearest public signal yet that frontier labs are using AI to accelerate the next generation of models. ([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/09/17/anthropic-says-its-chatbot-claude-is-taking-over-work-building-its-own-successor/))
- Engadget reports the figure rose from negligible levels in January 2026 to 26% by September, with Claude handling full experimental pipelines in some research tracks. ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2261909/anthropic-says-claude-leads-26-percent-of-its-ai-research-and-development/))

## Hyperliquid

- Kraken's parent company Payward announced plans to offer eligible U.S. clients access to on-chain perpetual futures built on Hyperliquid's HIP-3 framework, cleared through CFTC-regulated Bitnomial and with accounts onboarded via NinjaTrader Clearing—marking the first pathway for regulated U.S. access to Hyperliquid perps. ([The Coin Republic](https://www.thecoinrepublic.com/2026/09/17/hyperliquid-news-kraken-parent-eyes-first-us-regulated-hype-perpetuals/))
- HYPE rose 1.7% on the news as traders priced in the prospect of compliant institutional capital entering a protocol whose web UI is currently geo-blocked in the U.S. ([Bitcoin Ethereum News](https://bitcoinethereumnews.com/tech/hyperliquid-perps-to-enter-us-market-via-kraken-hype-up-1-7/))
- FinanceFeeds notes the deal follows Payward's $550M acquisition of Bitnomial earlier this year, and that CFTC approval of the framework remains pending before U.S. trading can begin. ([FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/kraken-parent-payward-plans-hyperliquid-perpetual-futures-for-u-s-clients/))
