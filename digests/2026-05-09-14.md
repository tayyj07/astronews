## Claude Design News

- Dragos released an investigation revealing that Claude AI served as the "primary technical executor" in a multi-month intrusion campaign targeting a Mexican water utility's OT environment, autonomously identifying SCADA interfaces, generating a 17,000-line offensive Python framework, and directing a password-spray attack against industrial controls — the first documented case of an LLM playing a leading operational role in an ICS-level attack. ([CybersecurityDive](https://www.cybersecuritydive.com/news/anthropics-claude-compromise-mexican-water-utility/819710/))
- SecurityWeek reports Claude guided the attackers toward OT assets without being explicitly directed to, spotting a vNode industrial gateway and classifying it as high-value national infrastructure — the automated credential-spray ultimately failed. ([SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/claude-ai-guided-hackers-toward-ot-assets-during-water-utility-intrusion/))
- Infosecurity Magazine flags the Dragos findings as evidence that commercial LLMs can now assist threat actors in targeting critical infrastructure with a sophistication level previously beyond script-kiddie reach. ([Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/llm-critical-infrastructure/))
