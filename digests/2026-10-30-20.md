## Cross-cutting themes

**The Binance hack as a stress test for stablecoin infrastructure.** The coordinated freeze by Tether, Circle, Ripple, and World Liberty Financial — covering $2.6B+ within hours — shows that regulated stablecoin issuers have built meaningful incident-response capacity. The gap: ~$2.86B in ETH and other non-freezable assets remains beyond reach, and attackers are actively routing through DeFi to exit. The episode highlights both the growing role of on-chain compliance rails and the structural limits where they break down.
