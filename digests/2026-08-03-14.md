## Cross-cutting themes

- **Regulatory arbitrage driving capital flows:** The South Korean stablecoin outflow story and the CLARITY Act deadlock both reflect the same structural gap — offshore regulatory environments offer products (derivatives, DeFi yield, staking) that domestic rules do not yet permit, accelerating cross-border stablecoin flows.
- **August deadlines in focus:** The Senate recess (August 7) and the CLARITY Act last-chance window (August 10) make this a pivotal week for U.S. crypto legislation, with knock-on implications for stablecoin issuers and TradFi banks building on-ramps.
