## Ethereum

- Vitalik Buterin formally proposed **EIP-8288**, a recursive STARK mempool framework for Ethereum's upcoming I-Star upgrade. Transactions will declare quantum-safe signatures as dependency frames, letting mempool nodes batch them into a single aggregate proof — potentially cutting quantum-safe private transaction costs from ~10 million gas to the low tens of thousands (>99% reduction). ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/eip-8288-recursive-stark-mempools-ethereum/))

- ETH slid to ~$2,465 Thursday morning as crypto markets pulled back ahead of PPI/CPI data due Thursday–Friday, the last inflation prints before the Fed's rate-setting meeting opens Tuesday. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/bitcoin-and-ethereum-prices-today-thursday-september-10-2026-crypto-prices-slide-back-with-inflation-data-on-tap-113018195.html))

## Stablecoins

- **MoneyGram launched its first stablecoin-backed Visa card**, initially available in Colombia. Built with Rain, the card lets users hold a USDC balance, pay anywhere Visa is accepted, and transfer to local cash pickup at MoneyGram locations; Apple Wallet / Google Wallet support is included at launch. MoneyGram's own MGUSD stablecoin will be added in a future update. A physical card with ATM access is planned for late 2026. ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-09-10-moneygram-card-stablecoin-visa-colombia-414137))
