## Solana

With Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, BlackRock, and Coinbase as anchor partners, this is one of the largest institutional endorsements of Solana as a settlement layer to date. ## Stablecoins

- **Open Standard unveiled Open USD (OUSD)**: a consortium-governed stablecoin backed by 140+ companies—Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, American Express, BlackRock, BNY Mellon, Coinbase, Google, Samsung, Shopify, DoorDash, OKX, Ripple, and Aave. OUSD charges no mint/redeem fees, imposes no volume caps, and returns nearly all reserve earnings to partners; governance is collective, not single-issuer. Circle shares fell ~12% on the news. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/06/30/circle-slides-8-as-stripe-coinbase-and-blackrock-back-rival-stablecoin-network))

- Fortune frames OUSD as the broadest-ever industry effort to unseat USDC and Tether, which together hold ~80% of the $300B+ stablecoin market; launch is targeted for later in 2026 natively on Solana. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/30/stripe-visa-stablecoin-rival-ousd-tether-circle/))

- The Defiant covers the governance innovation: Open Standard is an independent company where all 140+ partners share governance and earn from reserve income after a small management fee, with zero issuance limits and zero user fees—a structural departure from how USDC and USDT operate. ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/converge/tradfi-and-fintech/open-standard-open-usd-ousd-stablecoin-consortium-blackrock-visa-mastercard))

- Cryptopolitan notes that Ripple, Google, and Samsung Electronics are among the 140+ signatories—widening the coalition well beyond pure crypto-native firms. ([Cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/coinbase-visa-blackrock-140-join-open-usd/))

## Stablecoins Development in the TradFi Space

([Unchained Crypto](https://unchainedcrypto.com/coinbase-visa-stripe-and-more-back-new-open-usd-stablecoin-in-challenge-to-circle-and-tether/))

## Cross-cutting themes

Today's dominant story is the Open USD launch—arguably the most consequential stablecoin announcement since Libra/Diem in 2019. Unlike Diem (which died under regulatory pressure), OUSD arrives with payments incumbents (Visa, Mastercard, Stripe) as founding members at a moment when US stablecoin legislation (GENIUS Act) is actively being implemented, giving it a regulatory tailwind its predecessors lacked. The choice of Solana as the launch chain cements Solana's position as the preferred institutional settlement layer—adding to a week that already included $1.36B in tokenized equity volume on the network. Meanwhile, Anthropic's Claude Science launch into pharma/biotech signals the company is moving from horizontal AI tooling toward vertical, domain-specific products as a path to revenue diversification.
