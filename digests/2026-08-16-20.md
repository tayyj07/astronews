## Ethereum

- Ethereum's Glamsterdam upgrade enters its final devnet phase, targeting a 200 million gas limit (up from ~60 million today). The hard fork bundles EIP-7732 (ePBS — on-chain block-building, up to 70% less MEV), EIP-7928 (Block-Level Access Lists enabling parallel execution at ~10,000 TPS), and EIP-7904 (~78.6% fee reduction). Mainnet activation is aspirationally targeted for end of August 2026, subject to cross-client testing. ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/blockchains/ethereum-glamsterdam-final-devnet-200m-gas-limit-target))

## Cross-cutting themes

- **Settlement-rail competition intensifying**: Wells Fargo, JPMorgan, and Citi are all racing to deploy tokenized-deposit products before stablecoin consortia (Open USD, Circle's Arc chain) lock in merchant and institutional distribution. The Clarity Act delay in the Senate leaves regulatory uncertainty, pushing both sides to move faster on live product launches.
- **Regulatory pathway for on-chain derivatives**: The CFTC's Kalshi HYPE approval signals a shift — on-chain perpetual futures are gaining a regulated US home, a dynamic that could benefit other DeFi protocols pursuing similar paths.
- **Ethereum and Solana both targeting throughput step-changes**: Glamsterdam's 200M gas limit + parallel execution and Solana's 200ms slot times (with 150ms Alpenglow on the horizon) are both arriving in the same ~6-week window, setting up an infrastructure-layer performance race heading into Q4 2026.
