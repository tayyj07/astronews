## Cross-cutting themes

Compute scarcity is reshaping AI company strategy: Anthropic's simultaneous deals with Akamai, SpaceX, and Google signal that frontier AI providers are treating compute access as a first-order strategic constraint, not just an infrastructure cost. Meanwhile, the stablecoin sector is transitioning from a "permission" story (GENIUS Act) to an execution story — infrastructure, privacy, and institutional distribution are the next battleground for both crypto-native firms and TradFi incumbents.
