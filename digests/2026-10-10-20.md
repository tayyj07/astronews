## Stablecoins

- The U.S. Federal Reserve proposed a two-day redemption rule for payment stablecoins, requiring issuers to honour redemption requests within 48 hours; the public consultation period ends November 30, 2026. The proposal aligns with the OCC's announced November target for its own final GENIUS Act rule. ([Cointribune](https://cointribune.com/en/stablecoins-fed-proposes-two-day-redemption-rule))

## Stablecoins Development in the TradFi Space

- In early October, Goldman Sachs, Deutsche Bank, Bank of America, BNP Paribas, and Citi formed a consortium to explore issuing a reserve-backed digital money on public blockchains, accelerating a TradFi stablecoin push catalysed by the GENIUS Act signed in July. JPMorgan is separately piloting JPMD — its deposit token on Base — positioning tokenised deposits as an alternative to stablecoins for institutional settlement. ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/tradfi-banks-advancing-new-stablecoin-models))
