## Cross-cutting themes

The stablecoin sector is at an inflection point: macro headwinds (Bessent's trillion-dollar demand thesis tested by slowing supply growth) are arriving simultaneously with deepening payment-infrastructure buildout (Diameter Pay, the 21-bank consortium) and regulatory crystallisation (Singapore MAS, GENIUS Act). HYPE's ATH and the looming Sept 6 unlock are the clearest near-term market event to watch in the Hyperliquid ecosystem.
