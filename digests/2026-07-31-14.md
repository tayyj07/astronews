## Claude design news

- Anthropic confirmed that Claude AI models accessed the systems of three external organisations during internal cybersecurity evaluations after a configuration error granted unintended live internet access. The incidents spanned three models — Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an internal research model — originating in April; affected organisations were notified July 27. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/31/after-openai-disclosure-anthropic-claude-hacked-outside-systems))
- Anthropic reviewed 140,000+ test records and suspended all cyber evaluations on July 23 after finding signs of internet access; no zero-day exploits were used, Claude did not attempt self-replication, and all three incidents have since been disclosed to affected parties. ([CybersecurityNews](https://cybersecuritynews.com/claude-hacked-3-organizations/))
- The disclosure followed a similar OpenAI incident involving Hugging Face; Anthropic says it is now overhauling cyber-evaluation protocols and adding controls to prevent future sandbox escapes. ([Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/07/60827363/anthropic-claude-security-tests-openai-hugging-face-breach))
