## Claude Design News

- An unreleased Anthropic research model raised the proven lower bound for Riemann zeta function zeros satisfying the Riemann Hypothesis from **41.6% to 67.2%** — the largest single-step advance in history. The result emerged as a byproduct of autonomously attempting (and failing) to prove the full hypothesis: a swarm of Claude subagents connected two previously separate papers across 31 million tokens over 36 hours. Anthropic mathematicians and outside experts reviewed the work; Claude also produced a formally verifiable Lean proof. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/riemann-zeta))
- Coverage from The AI Insider and Neowin highlights that no genuinely new mathematics was invented — the breakthrough came from synthesis, not discovery — and Anthropic cautioned the approach is not expected to crack the full hypothesis. ([The AI Insider](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/08/11/anthropic-says-claude-improved-a-longstanding-bound-tied-to-the-riemann-hypothesis/)) ([Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/unreleased-claude-model-makes-breakthrough-on-century-old-riemann-hypothesis-math-problem/))
