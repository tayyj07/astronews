## Solana

- Continued post-mortems on the August 12 TeraSwitch routing fault: 102 of 699 validators stopped voting for up to 33 minutes, bringing the network to within 4.5 percentage points of losing transaction finality; Marinade announced it will tighten per-ASN and per-data-centre concentration limits in response. ([AMBCrypto](https://ambcrypto.com/solana-outage-knocks-90-validators-offline-was-the-network-at-risk/))
- New count confirms 102 validators went offline during the incident while the other 597 kept the network live; block production and finalisation continued throughout. ([Crypto.news](https://crypto.news/solana-stays-online-as-102-of-699-validators-stop-voting/))
- Coinpaprika's reconstruction of the event notes that AS20326 (TeraSwitch) hosts ≈27.34% of staked SOL, making it the single largest autonomous-system risk concentration on the network. ([Coinpaprika](https://coinpaprika.com/news/solana-neared-transaction-halt-33-minute/))
