## ethereum

- Ethereum developers have confirmed **October 6** as the target date for the Glamsterdam upgrade on the **Sepolia testnet**, with teams required to have code ready by September 29. ## solana

- Solana's **Alpenglow** upgrade is on the final countdown to its **September 28** mainnet-beta activation. General validators were recommended to upgrade by September 21 — today — with the feature-activation epoch expected a week later. The upgrade targets 150 ms finality, a 99% improvement over the current ~12.8 s. ([Finance BigGo](https://finance.biggo.com/news/936f36ab-aab7-4a60-9954-3df71d5a645b))

## stablecoins

- **Western Union** has officially launched **USDPT**, a USD-backed stablecoin issued by federally regulated Anchorage Digital Bank, built on Solana. The company plans to use USDPT for global exchange listings, institutional settlement, and a consumer product called "Stable by Western Union" rolling out to over 40 countries through 2026, with initial operations in the Philippines and Bolivia. ([Western Union](https://ir.westernunion.com/news/archived-press-releases/press-release-details/2026/Western-Union-Launches-USDPT-on-Solana-Advancing-Regulated-Digital-Infrastructure-for-Global-Payments/default.aspx))

## stablecoins development in the tradfi space

- A consortium of **21 major international financial institutions** has announced formation of a new company to issue a USD-denominated stablecoin, targeting a **H1 2027** market launch and an eventual EUR stablecoin. The group represents the third major bank-led stablecoin effort in 90 days, following Open USD (Visa, Mastercard, Stripe) in June and the BankChain Alliance (39 state banks) in August. ([PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/group-of-leading-international-financial-institutions-to-establish-stablecoin-enterprise-302866318.html))
