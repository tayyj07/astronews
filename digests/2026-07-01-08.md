## Cross-cutting themes

**MiCA enforcement day coincides with the OUSD launch**: July 1 is simultaneously the moment the EU's 18-month stablecoin grace period expires and the day OUSD's broader coverage crystallises the scale of the rival coalition to Tether and USDC. The timing reinforces a structural stablecoin shift: as USDT becomes non-compliant in Europe and Binance exits EU markets unlicensed, a regulated, partner-governed stablecoin with TradFi anchor investors is stepping into the vacuum.
