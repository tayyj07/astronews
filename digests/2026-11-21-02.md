## Ethereum

- Ethereum's Glamsterdam hard fork has been pushed into 2027, with mid-January now the floor and May 2027 on the table if developers alter gas-repricing proposals EIP-8037 and EIP-8038 to avoid breaking an estimated 15,000 existing contracts; no final schedule has been set. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/11/20/ethereum-s-next-big-upgrade-is-pushed-into-2027-by-apps-it-would-break))
- Cointelegraph reports a May 2027 scenario is now being discussed at the core-dev level, with client teams expected to file positions within two weeks and a decision expected at the November 26 All Core Developers call. ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/ethereum-glamsterdam-could-slip-to-may-2027))
- CoinCentral frames the delay as developers choosing app-safety over speed, noting that ChainSecurity and Dedaub have already contacted 50+ affected projects. ([CoinCentral](https://coincentral.com/ethereums-glamsterdam-upgrade-slips-into-2027-as-developers-fix-apps-that-could-break))

## Hyperliquid

- CFTC Chair Michael Selig laid out two licensing paths for Hyperliquid's US market entry at the Capitol Crypto Forum: register as a DCM/DCO/FCM exchange (citing Backpack as proof it is viable) or partner with an already-registered platform; no special blockchain exemptions will be granted. ([Crypto News](https://crypto.news/cftc-hyperliquid-us-market-regulatory-pathways))
- KuCoin summarises Selig's remarks, noting Kraken-parent Payward is exploring HIP-3 perpetual markets routed through CFTC-registered Bitnomial as a potential partnership model. ([KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/cftc-outlines-two-routes-for-hyperliquid-s-u-s-market-entry))

## Stablecoins
