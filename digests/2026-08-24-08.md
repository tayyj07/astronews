## Solana

- Solana activated SIMD-0525 at epoch 1020, cutting its target slot time from 400 ms to 350 ms — the first slot time reduction since network launch. ([Crypto News](https://crypto.news/solana-cuts-slot-time-to-350ms-for-first-time-since-network-launch/))
