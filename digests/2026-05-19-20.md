## Claude Design News

- Anthropic shipped two new enterprise capabilities for Claude Managed Agents: **self-hosted sandboxes** let companies execute agent tool calls on their own infrastructure (or through Cloudflare, Modal, Vercel, Daytona) rather than Anthropic's, while **MCP tunnels** connect agents to private-network MCP servers without exposing any public endpoints — traffic is end-to-end encrypted via a single outbound gateway connection. Self-hosted sandboxes enter public beta; MCP tunnels launch in research preview. ([Claude Blog](https://claude.com/blog/claude-managed-agents-updates))
- Coverage from The Decoder details the architectural split: orchestration and context management stay on Anthropic's infrastructure; only tool execution moves to the self-hosted environment — full on-premise is not yet possible. ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-adds-self-hosted-sandboxes-and-mcp-tunnels-to-claude-managed-agents/))
