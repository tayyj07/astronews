## Claude design news

- Anthropic launched **Claude Dashboards** and **Claude Motion** in beta on October 8. Dashboards connects to data warehouses (BigQuery, Databricks, Snowflake) and CRMs, builds charts that stay current as source data changes, and exposes each query for inspection. Motion generates short, editable animations from reports or slide decks — output is code, not AI-generated video — and exports to MP4 with push options to Adobe, Canva, Runway, and Luma AI. Dashboards is available on all paid plans; Motion is limited to Team and Enterprise. Docs, Slides, and Design simultaneously graduated from beta to all plans, including Free. ([Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/claude-can-now-build-live-dashboards-and-animated-explainers-from-your-data/))
- The standalone claude.ai/design site remains up through December 14, 2026; Design now also lives inside Claude conversations. Project chats and public links from the standalone version will not carry over. ([CellCog](https://cellcog.ai/blog/claude-dashboards-motion/))

## Solana

- Solana DEX Orca and credit protocol Loopscale have merged into a new entity called **Formation**, focused on financing AI, robotics, energy, and defense assets. Loopscale co-founder Luke Truitt is CEO; Orca's Christopher Montagano is chief strategy and legal officer. Formation plans to launch a tokenized securities venue under the SEC's Innovation Exemption, with Figure, Shinhan Asset Management, Superstate, R3, and Securitize as named partners. Orca reports $550 billion in trading volume since 2021; Loopscale reports $150 million in deposits and $2 billion in loans facilitated. Both protocols remain live, and ORCA/xORCA tokens are unchanged. ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/380522/solana-defi-firms-orca-and-loopscale-merge-under-new-formation-brand))

## Stablecoins

- **ESMA** directed EU-authorized crypto firms on October 8 to cease servicing stablecoins that do not comply with MiCA. National regulators must require outstanding exposures to be wound down "as soon as possible, and no later than January 8, 2027." The order covers trading, exchange, custody, portfolio management, and order-execution services. Tether's USDT and PayPal's PYUSD are the most widely cited affected tokens. Limited wind-down services (liquidation, conversion, withdrawal) are permitted on a temporary, supervised basis. ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/esma-unauthorised-stablecoins-three-month-deadline))
- CoinDesk: EU platforms may restrict access before January 8 — the January date is the outer regulatory limit, not a guarantee of continued service until then. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/10/08/eu-securities-regulator-gives-crypto-platforms-3-months-to-remove-unauthorized-stablecoins))
