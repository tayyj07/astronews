## Claude Design News

- Anthropic redeployed **Claude Fable 5** globally on July 1 after the U.S. Commerce Department lifted the June 12 export control order; the ban was triggered by a jailbreak found by Amazon researchers that coaxed the model into writing exploit code. Pro, Max, Team, and select Enterprise users get up to 50% of weekly usage limits in Fable 5 through July 7, after which it shifts to usage credits. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5))
- Alongside the redeployment, Anthropic added a new safety classifier targeting the specific jailbreak technique (blocks it in >99% of cases, at the cost of more false positives on routine coding tasks) and is co-proposing an industry-wide jailbreak severity scoring framework with Amazon, Microsoft, Google, and Glasswing partners. ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/anthropic-restores-claude-fable-5-after.html))
- VentureBeat examines what the Fable 5 return means for enterprise builders, noting the new classifier's higher false-positive rate on coding and debugging requests as the key trade-off. ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-is-bringing-back-claude-fable-5-globally-after-us-lifts-export-control-order-where-can-enterprises-access-it))
- NBC News on the U.S. Commerce Department giving the green light for Anthropic to restore Fable 5, framing it as a landmark moment in government oversight of commercial AI capabilities. ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/commerce-department-gives-green-light-anthropic-bring-back-fable-5-rcna352501))
- Digital Trends on how Fable 5 returns with a temporary usage cap, and what users on lower plans can expect. ([Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/youll-be-able-to-use-claude-fable-5-again-starting-july-1/))
