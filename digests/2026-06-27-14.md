## Hyperliquid

- Singapore's **MAS added Hyperliquid to its Investor Alert List**, flagging it as an entity not licensed by MAS — the same list that previously captured KuCoin (Feb 2026) and Bybit (June 17). The move is a consumer-protection notice, not a ban or enforcement action. ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/hyperliquid-singapore-mas-investor-alert-list/))
- Hyperliquid responded by reiterating it has **never claimed MAS authorisation**, that user self-custody and full on-chain settlement are unchanged, and that permissionless infrastructure remains intact. ([AMBCrypto](https://ambcrypto.com/hyperliquid-says-mas-alert-list-is-not-an-enforcement-action-after-singapore-warning/))
- **HYPE recovered ~7%** as markets absorbed the MAS news, trading around $64–$65; the token broke out of a descending channel on the 4-hour chart with a bullish MACD crossover and RSI back above 50. ([TradersUnion](https://tradersunion.com/news/cryptocurrency-news/show/2497153-hyperliquid-gains-7-02percent-to-usd64-6/))
