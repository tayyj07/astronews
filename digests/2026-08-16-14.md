**Regulated US venues are becoming the gateway for on-chain assets.** The Kalshi/CFTC HYPE approval and the bank tokenised-deposit race share the same underlying dynamic: Wall Street infrastructure is being rebuilt to accommodate crypto-native assets and instruments, with regulatory compliance as the threshold.
