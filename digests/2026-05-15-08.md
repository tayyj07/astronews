## Stablecoins

- The Clarity Act cleared the Senate Banking Committee 15-9 on May 14 with bipartisan support (Democrats Gallego and Alsobrooks crossing party lines), advancing to a full Senate floor vote; Coinbase surged 8% and Galaxy Digital gained 6.3% on the news. ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5878630-senate-crypto-regulation-bill/))
- The bill still needs 60 Senate floor votes; an unresolved ethics provision around elected officials holding crypto — and Alsobrooks' pledge not to support the floor vote until it is fixed — remains the primary obstacle. ([Bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/pro-crypto-clarity-act-h-r-3633-passes-senate-banking-committee-15-9/))
