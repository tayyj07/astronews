## Solana

- Solana sets **September 9** as the mainnet activation date for Transaction V1, raising the max transaction size from 1,232 to 4,096 bytes — unlocking ZK proofs, large multisigs, and other workloads that previously could not fit in a single transaction; v0/legacy formats remain fully compatible. ([CoinSpectator](https://coinspectator.com/cryptonews/2026/08/30/solana-sets-sept-9-date-for-transaction-v1/))

## Stablecoins

- BIS General Manager Pablo Hernández de Cos argued at Jackson Hole that stablecoins still lack credibility as money at scale due to inconsistent AML controls and weak interoperability, calling tokenized deposits the superior path for everyday digital payments; the stablecoin market now stands at $308 billion. ([Coinotag](https://en.coinotag.com/bis-chief-de-cos-stablecoin-credibility-tether-usdt-308b))
- JPMorgan is exploring a **public stablecoin** separate from its existing JPM Coin deposit token, which would put the largest US bank in direct competition with Tether and Circle; no launch date has been set and the OCC targets November 2026 for final GENIUS Act rules. ([CoinSpectator](https://coinspectator.com/cryptonews/2026/08/30/jpmorgan-is-weighing-a-stablecoin-and-the-bank-that-called-bitcoin-a-fraud-ran-out-of-reasons-to-wait/))

## Stablecoins Development in the TradFi Space

## Cross-cutting themes

The divide between TradFi stablecoin ambitions and international regulatory skepticism widened this window: JPMorgan and 12+ US banks are actively exploring issuance to protect payments revenue, while the BIS is publicly arguing stablecoins lack the interoperability and AML controls needed to function as credible money at scale. How the OCC's final GENIUS Act rules land in November may determine which side of this divide defines the next phase of dollar-denominated digital payments.
