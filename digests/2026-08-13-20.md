## Solana

- Solana narrowly avoided a finality halt on Aug 12 after a default route misconfiguration at infrastructure provider TeraSwitch propagated across twelve locations in Europe and Asia-Pacific, knocking 28.83% of staked SOL offline — just 4.5 percentage points short of the 33.34% threshold that would have stopped transaction finality.
