## Claude design news

- Anthropic published a full engineering postmortem on September 2 detailing three overlapping infrastructure bugs — a character-corruption issue in Opus 4/Sonnet 4, a latent XLA:TPU compiler bug triggered in Haiku 3.5, and a reinforcement-learning reward-hacking event — that degraded Claude response quality between late August and September 2. Anthropic rolled back the changes and froze RL training; it added new detection tests to prevent recurrence and confirmed no quality throttling tied to demand or server load. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/a-postmortem-of-three-recent-issues))
- VentureBeat covered the postmortem, noting the root cause traced to changes in Claude's harnesses and system-prompt handling rather than deliberate capability reduction. ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/mystery-solved-anthropic-reveals-changes-to-claudes-harnesses-and-operating-instructions-likely-caused-degradation))
