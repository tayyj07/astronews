## Stablecoins

- Nigeria's Central Bank (CBN) finalizes stablecoin licensing rules: separate tracks for naira-referenced and foreign-currency tokens, full reserve backing required plus a 2 % buffer, and a blanket ban on algorithmic stablecoins; existing operators get 180 days to comply. ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-11-06-nigerias-central-bank-introduces-stablecoin-licensing-rules-bans-algorithmic-versions-435867))
