## Solana

- Alpenglow officially activated on Solana mainnet at slot 459,653,000 (~14:00 UTC Oct 21), retiring TowerBFT. Anza's SolanaCompass post details the mechanics: the 150 ms finality target is achieved through Votor's fast-path (96% of blocks) with a slow-path fallback; validator admission shifts to a flat 0.8 SOL per-epoch fee, cutting estimated costs by ~60%. Transaction count metrics will temporarily look lower as vote transactions disappear from on-chain counts. ([SolanaCompass](https://solanacompass.com/news/solana-alpenglow-is-live-on-mainnet-as-anza-retires-towerbft-at-slot-459653000))
- Cointelegraph's post-launch summary notes that Alpenglow is the most ambitious Solana upgrade since the 2020 mainnet launch, with the second component (Rotor, the block-propagation layer) still pending a separate activation date. ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/solanas-alpenglow-launches-mainnet-99-faster-finality))
- Anza's official PRNewswire release calls Alpenglow "the world's fastest finality on a global network," citing sub-second confirmation on a permissionless validator set; SOL traded near $107 at activation. ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/alpenglow-is-live-on-solana-the-worlds-fastest-finality-on-a-global-network-302915579.html))

## Stablecoins

- The OCC's GENIUS Act final rule (issued Oct 21) sets a January 18, 2027 effective date. Genfinity highlights a contested detail: no safe harbor for existing stablecoin rewards programmes — issuers cannot pay yield purely for holding, and third-party rewards face case-by-case review. Existing issuers may apply for a 12-month waiver. ([Genfinity](https://genfinity.io/2026/10/21/occ-genius-act-final-rule-stablecoin-rewards-no-safe-harbor/))
- Bitcoin.com notes the OCC rule leaves stablecoin yield limits deliberately unresolved, pushing that debate to a future rulemaking with Treasury; AML/BSA and sanctions requirements are excluded from this rule. ([Bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/occ-finalizes-genius-act-rules-as-stablecoin-yield-faces-limits/))
- Crypto.news reports Circle and Coinbase shares rose more than 4% on the final rule release, seeing it as regulatory clarity that clears a path for compliant US stablecoin issuance ahead of the January deadline. ([Crypto.news](https://crypto.news/genius-act-rules-finalized-by-occ-ahead-of-january-deadline/))

## Stablecoins in TradFi

- OCC Bulletin 2026-52, issued alongside the final rule, provides revised reporting forms and instructions for permitted payment stablecoin issuers under OCC jurisdiction — the compliance paperwork layer that regulated banks and trust companies will need ahead of January 2027. ([OCC](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2026/bulletin-2026-52.html))
