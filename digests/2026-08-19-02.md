## Stablecoins

- **GENIUS Act rules hit the Federal Register**: The US Treasury's NPRM on Payment Stablecoin Issuance, Offer, and Sale was formally published in the Federal Register on August 18 — a 60-day public comment period opens today, closing October 19. Issuers must hold federal or state licenses by January 18, 2027; non-compliance carries fines up to $1M and up to 5 years in prison.
