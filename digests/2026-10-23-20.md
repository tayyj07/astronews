## Stablecoins

- Banking industry groups are pushing back on the OCC's final GENIUS Act rules, arguing the shift to a case-by-case review of third-party reward arrangements creates a loophole that lets crypto-native issuers effectively pay yield while bank issuers cannot — widening the competitive gap. ([Parameter](https://parameter.io/banking-industry-warns-final-stablecoin-rules-create-loopholes-for-yield-payments/))
- Stablecoin Insider publishes a detailed breakdown of OCC Bulletin 2026-50 — reserve requirements (1:1, allowable assets), two-business-day redemption rule, audit obligations, and the yield ban's scope and carve-outs. ([Stablecoin Insider](https://stablecoininsider.org/occ-genius-act-final-rule/))
- CoinPedia covers the split reaction: stablecoin-native issuers welcome the dropped yield-presumption restriction, while the American Bankers Association warns the rule will accelerate deposit flight to permissionless platforms. ([CoinPedia](https://coinpedia.org/crypto-live-news/genius-act-news-occ-final-rule-drops-stablecoin-yield-curbs-banks-push-back/))
