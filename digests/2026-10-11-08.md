## Ethereum

- Thailand's Securities and Exchange Commission finalized rules permitting Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs on the Stock Exchange of Thailand, effective October 16, 2026. ETFs must maintain at least 80% net exposure to a single crypto asset; margin lending for these products is prohibited. No fund has yet filed to launch. ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-10-09-thailand-sec-issues-bitcoin-and-ether-etf-rules-set-to-take-effect-oct-16-418132))
- U.S. spot Ethereum ETFs extended their losing streak — not a single inflow day logged in October so far, with BlackRock driving a $56M daily net outflow. Bitcoin ETFs meanwhile saw inflows. ([CoinGabbar](https://www.coingabbar.com/en/crypto-etf-news-today-bitcoin-inflows-ethereum-solana-outflows))
