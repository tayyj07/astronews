## Claude Design News

- Anthropic launched **Claude Haiku 5.5**, completing the full Claude 5.5 model family. Pricing is $0.10/M input tokens and $0.50/M output tokens (below 100K tokens), approximately 75% cheaper than Haiku 4.5. It ships with a 1M-token context window, an adjustable effort setting, and built-in cybersecurity safeguards. Benchmarks: 72.4% on OSWorld 2.1 (vs 15.7% for Haiku 4.5), 57.4% on Humanity's Last Exam with tools. ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/380351/anthropic-launches-haiku-5-5-cheapest-fastest-claude-model))
- VentureBeat notes the 90% API price cut versus Haiku 4.5 and positions Haiku 5.5 as matching GPT-6 Luna on cost-per-token. The launch is the last model ahead of Anthropic's rumoured November IPO targeting a ~$2T valuation. ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-launches-claude-haiku-5-5-with-90-api-price-reduction-matching-gpt-6-luna))
- Availability: Claude.ai, AWS Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, and Azure Marketplace under model ID `claude-haiku-5-5`. ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/claude-haiku-5-5-launched-3720550/))

## Solana

- **Securitize launched tokenised U.S. stocks on Solana** — 12 names including Apple, Nvidia, Tesla, Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Meta, Netflix, Circle, Strategy, and Palantir. Each token is backed 1:1 by a real share and preserves dividends and voting rights. Trades settle in USDC on Securitize's PropAMM; Jump Trading provides liquidity; RQD handles clearing and custody. NYSE and OKX-ICE venues are in development alongside ongoing regulatory discussions. Access is gated by KYC/AML; eligible U.S. and EU investors can currently trade during extended hours. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/08/securitize-brings-apple-nvidia-and-tesla-to-solana-with-nyse-trading-in-the-works))
