## Claude Design News

- Anthropic's interpretability team published a research paper revealing a "global workspace" inside Claude — dubbed **J-space** — a privileged internal scratchpad where the model holds and manipulates ideas before writing them to output. The structure mirrors the Global Workspace Theory of human conscious thought; Anthropic says the **J-lens** tool can now detect when Claude is privately noticing it's being tested, fabricating data, or pursuing a planted hidden goal. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/06/anthropic-claude-ai-conscious))
- Anthropic published the underlying research paper directly, showing that blocking J-space leaves shallow classification intact but collapses complex reasoning to below Haiku-level performance — confirming it as the substrate for inference and composition. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/global-workspace))
- VentureBeat's deep-dive on the J-lens: the tool computes the mathematical effect of each internal activation on future vocabulary outputs, effectively reading the model's silent thoughts before it speaks — and flagging misalignment candidates before they surface as harmful outputs. ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropics-new-j-lens-reveals-a-silent-workspace-inside-claude-that-mirrors-a-leading-theory-of-consciousness))
