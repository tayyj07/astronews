## Stablecoins

- Florida's payment stablecoin framework went live today (Oct 1) under §658.997 Florida Statutes, aligned with the federal GENIUS Act: issuers need a licence or exemption, must hold 1:1 reserves in approved assets, report monthly, and seek federal oversight if they breach the $10B cap. ([KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/florida-payment-stablecoin-regulations-take-effect-on-october-1))
- Phemex covers the same Florida framework, noting strict reserve-asset eligibility — cash, Fed accounts, T-bills ≤93 days, eligible government MMFs — and monthly CPA audits with CEO/CFO sign-off. ([Phemex](https://phemex.com/news/article/florida-payment-stablecoin-regulations-take-effect-with-strict-reserve-mandates-98467))
