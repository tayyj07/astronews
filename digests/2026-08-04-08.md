## Stablecoins Development in the TradFi Space

- Mastercard officially closed its $1.8B acquisition of stablecoin infrastructure firm BVNK on August 3, adding $30B in annual stablecoin payment volume and becoming the first major publicly listed payment network to buy into stablecoin rails outright rather than partner into them.
