## Stablecoins

- Industry and analyst reaction to the Bank of England's draft framework framed the eased £40B issuance cap and scrapped individual holding limits as a turning point: BoE Deputy Governor Breeden's remarks that the bank "wants UK stablecoins to work" were widely quoted as a commitment to UK competitiveness in digital payments. ([BanklessTimes](https://www.banklesstimes.com/articles/2026/06/22/bank-of-england-softens-stablecoin-rules-scraps-individual-holding-caps/))
- CoinDesk analysis highlighted the reserve regime — 30% non-interest-bearing BoE deposits, 70% in short-term UK gilts — as materially improving stablecoin economics versus earlier proposals and setting a template distinct from the US GENIUS Act's approach. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/06/22/bank-of-england-backs-down-on-strict-stablecoin-holding-limits-sets-usd50-billion-issuance-cap))
- MoonPay acquires Entendre, an AI-agent accounting startup whose platform reconciles digital-asset transaction flows against major general ledgers (NetSuite, QuickBooks, Xero) and spend tools (Ramp, Stripe) for stablecoin-focused fintechs; the deal extends MoonPay's stack into automated finance operations. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/pro/fintech-deals/2026/06/22/moonpay-entendre-ai-accounting-stablecoins))

## Stablecoins Development in the TradFi Space

- The BoE's 30%/70% reserve split is seen by TradFi observers as a yields-friendly design that makes sterling stablecoin issuance viable for banks and fintechs: issuers can earn gilts yield on 70% of reserves while satisfying the systemic-risk guardrail, a structure closer to money-market-fund economics than the blanket non-interest models initially proposed. ([CryptoTimes](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/06/22/bank-of-england-eases-stablecoin-rules-with-40b-issuance-cap/))
- MoonPay's Entendre acquisition signals that enterprise-grade AI accounting infrastructure is converging with stablecoin payment rails, with Entendre's integrations spanning digital-asset banks (Rain, Meow, Slash) and traditional spend platforms — an early sign of the operational layer that TradFi stablecoin issuers will need. ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/moonpay-acquires-entendre-bringing-agentic-finance-to-the-stablecoin-economy-302805927.html))
