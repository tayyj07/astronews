## Cross-cutting themes

- **Japan stablecoin race**: Ripple (RLUSD + SBI) and Circle (USDC + Nomura) have both targeted Japan's institutional FX market within days of each other, setting up a direct competition for dollar-stablecoin infrastructure in Asia.
