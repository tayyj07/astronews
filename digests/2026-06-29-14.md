## Cross-cutting themes

Regulatory pressure on both stablecoins and DeFi is intensifying simultaneously: the GENIUS Act rulemaking machine is running (FDIC, OCC, Fed, NCUA, FinCEN all engaged), while Singapore MAS's Hyperliquid alert — amplified by CZ's decentralization warning — signals that regulators are sharpening focus on the legal status of "permissionless" protocols.
