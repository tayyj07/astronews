## Stablecoins

- Bank of England economist Megan Greene stated tokenized deposits are "probably going to take over from stablecoins," predicting that in five years people will wonder why stablecoins were being discussed at all; BoE draft systemic stablecoin rules are expected to publish in June 2026. ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/boe-tokenized-deposits-replace-stablecoins/))
- BoE confirms it has shifted its stablecoin draft ruleset to June, targeting a year-end final framework; key change: individual £20,000 holding caps dropped in favor of aggregate issuance limits placed on token providers, aiming to lower compliance costs and level the field with MiCA and US rules.
