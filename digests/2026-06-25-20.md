## Stablecoins

- **Ripple** took an equity stake in Flutterwave's Series E (valuation ~$3B) to embed RLUSD stablecoin and Ripple Payments across Africa's largest cross-border payment network, covering 1B+ annual transactions — described as the largest single stablecoin settlement deployment in Africa's history. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/ripple-takes-stake-flutterwave-stablecoin-142400121.html))
- Analysis highlights that the **GENIUS Act (US)** and **MiCA (EU)** create incompatible stablecoin compliance regimes — no mutual recognition, separate licences required, divergent reserve rules (GENIUS: T-bills/cash; MiCA: 60% EU bank deposits) — putting multi-jurisdiction issuers under double compliance costs ahead of both frameworks' July deadlines. ([CoinPaprika](https://coinpaprika.com/news/mica-genius-act-incompatible-stablecoin/))

## Stablecoins — TradFi Development

- **JPMorgan, Citi, Bank of America, Wells Fargo** and other major US banks confirmed plans for a joint **tokenized deposit network** through The Clearing House (TCH), targeting a mid-2027 launch to provide a bank-native digital dollar alternative to third-party stablecoins; the initiative positions deposit tokens — insured, interest-bearing, KYC-gated — as the institutional-grade answer to USDC and Tether. ([American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/stablecoins-will-be-a-key-element-of-banking-infrastructure-in-2026))

## Cross-cutting themes

**South Korea as Solana's institutional proving ground:** Both the Toss Bank and KG Inicis MOUs point to South Korea fast-tracking real-world Solana stablecoin deployment at scale — Toss Bank's 15M users and KG Inicis' 220,000 merchants collectively represent a meaningful volume test for the network's settlement infrastructure.

**Deposit tokens vs. stablecoins crystallising:** JPMorgan's JPMD (already live on Base) plus the multi-bank TCH network announce a deliberate strategy to pull institutional settlement demand away from stablecoin issuers. The tension between bank-issued deposit tokens and permissionless stablecoins is becoming the defining structural question for TradFi crypto adoption in H2 2026.
