## Claude Design News

- Anthropic shipped a major Claude Design overhaul: users can import design systems from GitHub repos or uploads, and Claude now builds and self-corrects against those brand guidelines before showing output. A `/design-sync` command pulls a codebase's design system in; `/design` creates and edits projects from the terminal. The update also reduces token burn by sharing limits across chat, Cowork, and Claude Code. ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-ships-major-claude-design-overhaul-with-design-system-imports-code-round-trips-and-a-fix-for-its-token-burning-problem))

- Claude Design will now enforce your brand's color tokens, typography, and component library across every generated mockup instead of defaulting to generic AI-style output — admins can lock down approved design systems org-wide. ([Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/claude-design-will-now-stick-to-your-brand-guidelines-instead-of-generic-ai-mockups/))

- TechRepublic covers how the redesign targets enterprise design and engineering teams specifically, with admin-controlled brand compliance and bidirectional code-round-trip sync between Claude Design and Claude Code. ([TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-anthropic-claude-design-overhaul-enterprise-teams/))
