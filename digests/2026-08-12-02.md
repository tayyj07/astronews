## Claude Design News

- Anthropic announced it will embed invisible watermarks in all Claude-generated text globally, starting with models released after August 2, to comply with the EU AI Act's Transparency Code — but applying the rule worldwide rather than only to EU users. Text carries an imperceptible statistical signal; files use the C2PA open standard. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/anthropic-says-it-will-watermark-text-generated-by-its-ai-models/))
- The watermark signals AI *processing*, not AI *authorship*: Claude's mark can appear on text it only edited or proofread, not wrote from scratch — Anthropic acknowledges finding its watermark doesn't prove Claude wrote the original material. ([City AM](https://www.cityam.com/claude-to-watermark-ai-slop-including-edited-human-writing/))
- Euronews notes the decision to apply EU-triggered compliance globally follows a now-familiar pattern: regulatory requirements designed for one jurisdiction get adopted everywhere by default. ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/08/11/eu-compliance-delivered-globally-anthropic-to-watermark-claudes-output-worldwide))
