## stablecoins

- Five US agencies (FinCEN, Fed, OCC, FDIC, NCUA) jointly proposed bank-style Customer Identification Program (KYC) requirements for payment stablecoin issuers under the GENIUS Act; comments due 60 days after Federal Register publication on June 22. ([CoinPaper](https://coinpaper.com/32073/us-stablecoin-issuers-face-new-kyc-rules-from-fed-and-fincen-push))

## stablecoins development in the tradfi space
