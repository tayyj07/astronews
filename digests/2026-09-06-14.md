## Stablecoins

- OCC Comptroller Jonathan Gould, speaking at the Wyoming Blockchain Symposium, committed to finalising GENIUS Act stablecoin rules by November 2026 — enabling the OCC to begin processing issuer applications in early 2027. The FDIC and NCUA are advancing parallel proposals on the same timeline. ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/occ-genius-act-stablecoin-rule-november/))
