## Stablecoins

- BIS Working Paper No. 1270 argues that current stablecoins function more like ETFs than money: prices routinely deviate from peg, redemptions can be slow or uncertain, and transfers settle off central-bank balance sheets. The paper also warns that dollar-pegged coins are accelerating dollarisation in emerging-market economies, eroding local monetary sovereignty and evading capital controls. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/06/29/bis-warns-stablecoins-are-more-like-etfs-than-actual-money-and-they-re-creating-fx-risk))

## Stablecoins — TradFi Development

- The FDIC published a Financial Institution Letter with its full proposed rulemaking requiring permitted payment stablecoin issuers to maintain Customer Identification Programs (CIP) under the GENIUS Act / Bank Secrecy Act framework. Comments are due ahead of the July 18 GENIUS Act rulemaking deadline across six federal agencies. ([FDIC](https://www.fdic.gov/news/financial-institution-letters/2026/notice-proposed-rulemaking-permitted-payment-stablecoin))

With the July 18 GENIUS Act deadline for six agencies approaching, this week represents a step-change in compliance burden for issuers — and a signal to TradFi partners about what "regulated stablecoin infrastructure" will actually require.
