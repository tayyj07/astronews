## Ethereum

- Vitalik Buterin unveiled the "Lean Ethereum" roadmap — the protocol's biggest planned overhaul since the 2022 Merge — targeting quantum resistance (via STARKs and post-quantum cryptography by ~2029), RISC-V as a potential long-term EVM successor, and native privacy as first-class protocol goals, across a 3–4 year timeline. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/07/06/vitalik-buterin-says-ethereum-is-preparing-its-biggest-rebuild-since-the-merge))
- The Block notes Buterin identified the upcoming Hegotá fork as likely Ethereum's last before the Lean era fully begins; the roadmap caps growth of current flexible state while adding more scalable state types with recursive STARK verification. ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/407176/vitalik-buterin-says-ethereums-next-rebuild-will-rival-the-merge-and-take-three-to-four-years))
- CryptoBriefing frames the plan as Ethereum's third major iteration — post-launch and post-Merge — with "privacy is no longer an afterthought; it is a first-class goal" as Buterin's headline statement. ([CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/vitalik-ethereum-quantum-rebuild-timeline/))
