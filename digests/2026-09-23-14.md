## Cross-cutting Themes

**Regulated rails, regulated issuers**: This window's stories share a single thread — institutional finance is not waiting for consensus or legislation. Anchorage + LayerZero lock OFT into a federally chartered bank, Visa + Reap push stablecoin settlement into card programs, and yesterday's OCC charters put AI-agent finance inside the banking perimeter. The question is shifting from "will TradFi adopt stablecoins?" to "which settlement standard wins?"
