## Solana

- **SGP-0002 passed** in dramatic fashion: validators approved doubling the disinflation rate (15% → 30%), accelerating Solana's terminal 1.5% inflation floor to 2029 instead of 2032, avoiding ~18.9M SOL of new issuance over six years. The margin was razor-thin—67.0% vs. the 66.67% quorum required. ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/376825/solana-sol-disinflation-vote-passes))
- Kraken and Galaxy Digital flipped their votes from "against" to "for" in the final hours, deciding the outcome; implementation still requires SIMD-0550 client coordination. ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/solana-sgp-0002-passes-with-176-million-sol-as-governance-debate-shifts-to-technical-execution/))
- **SGP-0001 (Solana Constitution)** ratified with 86% support; **SGP-0003** (resource and inclusion fee burn) rejected at 53.9%, well short of the 66.67% threshold. ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/solana-validators-approve-proposal-to-accelerate-sol-disinflation))
- Yahoo Finance rounds up the narrow pass: 176.29M SOL for, 66.19M against, on 1,326 validators. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/solana-narrowly-passes-double-disinflation-163833464.html))
