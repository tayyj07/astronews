## Solana

- Morgan Stanley filed second amended S-1s for its proposed spot SOL and ETH ETFs, disclosing a 0.14% sponsor fee — the lowest proposed fee for either product in the US market. The filings also confirm a staking component using Figment, Galaxy Blockchain Infrastructure, and Coinbase Canada as providers, and represent active back-and-forth dialogue with the SEC. ([CoinPaper](https://coinpaper.com/32061/morgan-stanley-files-new-amendments-for-eth-and-sol-etfs))

## Stablecoins

- Five US agencies (FinCEN, OCC, Federal Reserve, FDIC, NCUA) jointly proposed a customer identification programme (KYC) rule for permitted payment stablecoin issuers under the GENIUS Act, published June 18. The proposal limits KYC obligations to direct-to-consumer relationships rather than requiring issuers to police all secondary-market transactions — a scoping decision that the industry had lobbied for. Public comment opens June 22 for 60 days. ([National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/fincen-and-banking-agencies-propose-customer-identification-program-rule-stablecoin))

## Stablecoins development in the tradfi space

- The U.S. Treasury published the official press release for the joint interagency GENIUS Act rulemaking, framing the KYC proposal as the implementation of the law's anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorism-financing requirements; this is the primary regulatory document that banks and fintechs planning stablecoin issuance will need to respond to in their comment letters by mid-August. ([U.S. Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0435))
