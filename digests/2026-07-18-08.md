## Stablecoins

- Today — July 18, 2026 — is the statutory deadline for all six federal agencies (OCC, FDIC, NCUA, Treasury, FinCEN, OFAC) to publish final GENIUS Act stablecoin rules; as of late July 17 no agency had released a finalized framework, raising questions about whether any will formally miss the congressionally mandated date. ([Launch Legal](https://launch-legal.com/articles/six-federal-agencies-face-a-critical-deadline-to-finalize-genius-act-stablecoin-rules))
- With the deadline arriving, mid-market stablecoin issuers face existential cost pressure: the OCC's draft sets a $5 M capital floor, a three-tier liquidity requirement, monthly independent audits, and a ban on yield payments, prompting payment firms such as Stripe and Block to weigh whether to charter a stablecoin bank or exit the market. ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/genius-act-deadline-puts-stablecoin-issuers-on-the-clock/))
