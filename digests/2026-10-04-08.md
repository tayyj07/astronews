## Ethereum

- The Ethereum Foundation and Open Anonymity Project launched **zkAPI** on Ethereum mainnet — a zero-knowledge proof system that lets users pay for metered AI-inference APIs without revealing their identity; users deposit credits into an on-chain vault once and authorise bounded usage with ZK proofs rather than account identity. ([Cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/ethereum-targets-ai-privacy-with-zkapi/))
- ETH short positions on Bitfinex have surged from ~771 ETH to over 101,000 ETH over two weeks, heightening short-squeeze watch; separately, ETH has flipped BTC in open interest on Hyperliquid — more capital now riding on ETH positions than BTC positions on that exchange. ([CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/9d772-ethereum-price-prediction-october-2026-eth-targets-3000-in-october-as-short-positions-stack-up))
